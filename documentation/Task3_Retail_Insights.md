# Task 3 — Retail Customer and Product Insights

## Overview

This task extends the retail analysis by examining relationships
between customers, products, quantities, prices, and revenue.

The objective is to extract useful business insights from the
transaction-level data.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Retail Sales Dataset

**File:** `retail_sales_dataset.csv`

## Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

1. Customer spending behavior.
2. Product-category performance.
3. Quantity purchased.
4. Revenue contribution.
5. Customer demographic patterns.
6. Relationships between numerical variables.

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed:

- Total revenue analysis.
- Average transaction amount.
- Category-wise revenue.
- Category-wise quantity.
- Gender-based purchasing analysis.
- Age-based analysis.
- Customer spending analysis.
- Correlation analysis.

## Visualization

Charts were created to visualize:

- Revenue by category.
- Quantity by category.
- Customer spending.
- Age-related purchasing patterns.
- Gender-based differences.
- Correlations between numerical variables.

## Business Insights

The analysis can help businesses understand:

- Which categories contribute most to revenue.
- Which products/categories have higher quantities.
- Customer spending patterns.
- Differences between customer demographics.
- Relationships between quantity, price, and total amount.

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Conclusion

Retail transaction data can provide valuable information about
customer behavior and product performance. The insights generated
from this task can support decisions related to marketing, product
planning, and sales strategy.
